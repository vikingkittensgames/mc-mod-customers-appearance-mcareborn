# Customers MCA Reborn Appearance

Customers MCA Reborn Appearance is an optional Fabric and NeoForge add-on for
[Customers](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/customers). It lets
Customer and Supplier Spawners use the Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn villager
appearance, including its models, genetics, clothing, hairstyles, voices, and
sitting behavior.

[Learn more about Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecraft-comes-alive-reborn).

## Use the appearance

Enable Minecraft Comes Alive in a Customer or Supplier Spawner's appearance
list. Spawned villagers use MCA Reborn's renderer while remaining Customers
entities, so they retain normal Customers gameplay.

The add-on preserves the original `customers:mca` appearance identifier.
Existing Customer and Supplier Spawners and villagers that used the former
built-in MCA appearance continue to use it after the add-on is installed.
